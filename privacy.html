YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ninjalens (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you visit ninjalens.net and use the tools available on this website (“Site”). Ninjalens is operated from Japan. For privacy questions, contact support@ninjalens.net.

1. Information we process

Website usage and analytics. If you choose “Accept analytics” in our consent notice, Google Analytics 4 (GA4) may process information about your visit, such as pages viewed, referring page, browser and device information, approximate location, and interactions with Site features. Our GA4 events may record that a tool was used and whether a sample or uploaded file was selected. We do not send CSV contents, product names, Handles, SKUs, file names, or the findings themselves to GA4.

Consent preference. We store your analytics choice in your browser’s local storage so we can remember it. This preference is not sent to us.

Technical request data. The hosting and delivery infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, request time, requested URL, browser information, and security events to deliver and protect the Site.

Messages to us. If you contact support, we process the contact details and message you choose to send so we can respond.

2. Shopify CSV tools

The Shopify CSV Checker reads the CSV files you select locally in your browser. The file contents are not uploaded to Ninjalens. The checker’s analytics events, when you have opted in, do not include CSV contents or product-level values. Your browser, extensions, or other services you use may handle files independently of Ninjalens.

3. Cookies and analytics choices

Optional GA4 analytics is off unless you choose to allow it. If enabled, Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies, including Google Analytics cookies, to distinguish visits and measure Site usage. If you reject analytics, we do not load the GA4 tag through this Site. You can change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer. You can also clear cookies and local storage in your browser.

4. Purposes and legal bases

We use information to operate and secure the Site, understand and improve feature usage where you have permitted optional analytics, and reply to support requests. Where required by applicable law, we rely on your consent for optional analytics and associated storage. You may withdraw that consent at any time using Cookie settings; withdrawal does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal.

5. Service providers and international processing

Google provides Google Analytics. If analytics is enabled, information is sent to Google for processing under Google’s terms and privacy documentation. Google may process information in countries other than the country where you are located. Our website hosting and delivery providers may also process technical request data. Their processing is subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy, Google Analytics safeguards and data practices, and the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

6. Retention and security

Analytics data is retained according to the settings of our GA4 property and Google’s applicable retention rules. Support messages are kept as long as reasonably needed to respond and maintain our records. Technical logs are retained according to the hosting provider’s settings and security needs. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no website or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

You can reject or withdraw optional analytics consent using Cookie settings. Depending on your location, applicable law may give you rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability of personal information. To make a request, contact support@ninjalens.net. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The Site is intended for general business use and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy as the Site or applicable requirements change. The revised date above indicates when the latest version was published.
